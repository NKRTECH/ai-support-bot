## What's Covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, including:

- Hardware failures not caused by physical damage
- Battery defects (if battery holds less than 50% of original capacity within warranty period)
- Display defects (dead pixels — 5 or more dead pixels qualifies for replacement)
- Component failures (motherboard, RAM, storage drive issues)
- Power adapter or charging cable defects

## What's NOT Covered

- Physical damage (drops, spills, cracks, bent connectors)
- Damage from unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Software issues, viruses, or data loss
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, fading, worn key labels)
- Damage from power surges or voltage fluctuations (use a surge protector or UPS!)
- Products purchased from unauthorized resellers
- Damage caused by using non-compatible third-party chargers

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Visit **smarttech.in/warranty** or call 1800-123-4567 (toll-free)
2. Enter your product serial number (found on the bottom of the device or in Settings > About)
3. Describe the issue in detail
4. Our support team will attempt remote troubleshooting first (via phone or AnyDesk)
5. If the issue can't be resolved remotely, choose one of:
   - **Doorstep service**: Our technician visits your location (available in 50+ cities)
   - **Courier pickup**: We send a courier to pick up your device (all India)
   - **Walk-in service**: Visit your nearest SmartTech Authorized Service Centre
6. Repairs typically take **7-10 business days**
7. Your product will be returned with free shipping

## Warranty Repairs vs. Replacement

- If the repair cost exceeds 70% of the product's current value, we'll **replace** the product instead of repairing it
- Replacement products may be new or refurbished units of equal or greater specifications
- If the exact model is discontinued, we'll offer the closest current equivalent
