## Payment Processing

- Payment is charged when your order is **confirmed** (for prepaid orders)
- For COD orders, payment is collected at the time of delivery (cash or UPI)
- Pre-authorization holds may appear on your card statement for 1-3 business days
- For pre-order items, your card is charged only when the item is ready to ship

## GST and Invoices

- All prices on smarttech.in are **inclusive of GST** (18% for electronics)
- GST invoice is generated automatically when your order ships
- All invoices are available in My Account > Orders > Order Details > Download Invoice
- **Business customers**: Add your GSTIN in My Account > Profile to get GST-compliant invoices for input tax credit
- If you need a revised invoice, contact support within 7 days of delivery

## EMI Options (No-Cost and Standard)

For orders over ₹5,000, we offer EMI through major banks:

### No-Cost EMI

| Tenure | Available On | Banks |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| 3 months | Orders ≥ ₹5,000 | HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak |
| 6 months | Orders ≥ ₹10,000 | HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis |
| 9 months | Orders ≥ ₹20,000 | HDFC, ICICI, SBI |
| 12 months | Orders ≥ ₹30,000 | HDFC, ICICI |

No-Cost EMI means the interest is borne by SmartTech — you pay only the product price split into equal monthly installments.

### Standard EMI

| Tenure | Interest Rate | Example (₹60,000 purchase) |
|--------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 3 months | 12% p.a. | ~₹20,300/month |
| 6 months | 13% p.a. | ~₹10,390/month |
| 12 months | 15% p.a. | ~₹5,420/month |
| 18 months | 16% p.a. | ~₹3,740/month |
| 24 months | 17% p.a. | ~₹2,920/month |

- EMI is available on credit cards from all major banks
- Debit card EMI is available for select HDFC, ICICI, and SBI cards
- Bajaj Finserv EMI cards are also accepted
- No prepayment penalty — you can foreclose EMI anytime via your bank

## Split Payments

You can split payment across:
- Store credit + UPI/card
- Gift card + UPI/card
- Store credit + gift card + UPI/card (up to 3 payment methods per order)

COD cannot be combined with other payment methods.

## Promotional Codes and Discounts

- Only **one promotional code** can be used per order
- Promotions cannot be applied retroactively to placed orders
- Some promotions exclude certain product categories (stated in promotion terms)
- Bank-specific offers (e.g., "10% off on HDFC cards") are applied automatically at checkout
- Coupons and bank offers can be stacked unless stated otherwise

## Billing Disputes

If you see an unrecognized charge from SmartTech:

1. Check My Account > Orders to verify it's not a recent order you forgot about
2. Check for pre-authorization holds that haven't cleared yet
3. If the charge is still unrecognized, contact support immediately
4. We will investigate within 2 business days
5. If the charge is confirmed fraudulent, we'll reverse it and secure your account
6. You can also raise a dispute with your bank/card issuer simultaneously
