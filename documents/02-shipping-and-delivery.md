## Order Processing

- Orders placed before **2:00 PM IST** on business days are processed the same day
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST or on weekends/public holidays are processed the next business day
- Custom-configured laptops take 3-5 additional business days for assembly before shipping
- During festival sales (Diwali Sale, Republic Day Sale), processing may take 1-2 extra days

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking SMS and email with:
- Tracking number (AWB number)
- Courier partner name (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, or Ecom Express)
- Estimated delivery date
- Link to track in real-time

You can also track orders by logging into your SmartTech account and visiting **My Orders**, or by sending your order number to our WhatsApp support at +91-98765-43210.

## Delivery Issues

### Package Not Received

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received the package:

1. Check with your society watchman/security, neighbors, or family members at home
2. Look for a delivery OTP SMS — our courier partners require OTP confirmation for high-value orders
3. Wait 24 hours, as tracking updates can sometimes be premature
4. If still not received, contact our support team with your order number

We will file a courier investigation and either **reship the item** or **issue a full refund** within 5 business days.

### Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong product:

1. Do **not** open or use the incorrect item
2. Contact support with your order number and a photo of the received item
3. We'll send the correct item with express shipping at no charge
4. Our courier partner will pick up the wrong item during the same visit (or separately)

### Package Arrived Damaged

See our [Return and Refund Policy](#) for instructions on damaged items. Report damage within 48 hours for fastest resolution. If the outer box is damaged at the time of delivery, you may **reject the delivery** and it will be automatically returned to us.

## Shipping Restrictions

The following items have shipping restrictions:
- Lithium battery products may have air shipping restrictions for certain northeast states
- Items weighing over 30 kg require special logistics (we'll contact you with options)
- Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to ₹50,000 only
- COD is not available for custom-configured laptops or pre-order items
