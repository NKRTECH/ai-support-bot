# Return and Refund Policy

**Category**: Returns  
**Last Updated**: July 2026  
**Applies To**: All SmartTech purchases

---

## 15-Day Return Window

All SmartTech products can be returned within **15 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange, as per Consumer Protection Act, 2019. The product must be in its original packaging with all accessories included.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your SmartTech account at smarttech.in/account
2. Go to **My Orders** and find the order
3. Click **"Request Return"** and select a reason
4. Schedule a **free pickup** from your address (available in 19,000+ pin codes)
5. Our courier partner will pick up within 2-3 business days

Alternatively, you can contact our support team at support@smarttech.in or call 1800-123-4567 (toll-free).

## Refund Processing Times

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---------------|----------------|
| UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) | 2-3 business days after we receive the item |
| Credit/Debit Card | 5-7 business days after we receive the item |
| Net Banking | 5-7 business days after we receive the item |
| SmartTech Store Credit | Instant upon return approval |
| EMI (cancelled) | 5-10 business days; EMI plan adjusted by your bank |
| Cash on Delivery orders | Refunded to your bank account (NEFT) — provide bank details |

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Products with broken seals on software or digital content
- Custom-configured laptops (built-to-order)
- Items marked as "Final Sale" on the product page
- Gift cards and digital download codes
- Items returned after the 15-day window

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your product arrives damaged or is defective:

1. Contact support within **48 hours** of delivery
2. Provide your order number and photos/video of the damage
3. We will schedule a **free reverse pickup** at no cost to you
4. Choose either a **full refund** or a **replacement** — your choice

Damaged item claims submitted after 48 hours may still be honored at our discretion, but we recommend reporting issues as soon as possible.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in the following situations:

- Item is returned with missing accessories (charger, cables, manuals)
- Item shows signs of use beyond basic inspection
- Original packaging is missing or damaged

The deduction amount depends on the missing items or condition. Our returns team will email you the adjusted refund amount before processing.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for a different variant, color, or model:

1. Initiate a return as described above
2. Select **"Exchange"** instead of **"Refund"**
3. Choose the replacement product
4. If the new item costs more, you'll be charged the difference
5. If it costs less, the difference is refunded to your original payment method

## Refunds for Orders Paid with Promotions or Discounts

If you used a coupon code or promotional discount:

- The refund amount will reflect the **price you actually paid**, not the MRP
- If a discount applied to the entire order (e.g., "20% off everything"), the discount is prorated across items
- Promotional store credits used will be returned as store credit, not cash
- GST refund is processed automatically with the product refund
