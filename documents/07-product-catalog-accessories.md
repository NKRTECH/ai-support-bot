## SmartTech USB-C Hub 7-in-1

**SKU**: ST-HUB7-2026

**Selling Price**: ₹3,499 (incl. GST)  
**Category**: Accessories

Ports provided:
- 1x HDMI 2.1 (supports 4K @ 60Hz)
- 2x USB-A 3.2 (up to 10 Gbps)
- 1x USB-C 3.2 (data only)
- 1x USB-C PD (100W pass-through charging)
- 1x SD card reader (UHS-II)
- 1x microSD card reader (UHS-II)

**Compatibility**: Works with any USB-C laptop or tablet. Optimized for SmartTech laptops but compatible with Apple MacBook, Dell, Lenovo, HP, and other USB-C devices.

**Troubleshooting — External monitor not displaying:**
1. Make sure the HDMI cable is plugged into the hub's HDMI port (not a USB-C port)
2. On Windows: press Win + P and select "Extend" or "Duplicate"
3. On macOS: System Settings > Displays > Detect Displays
4. Try a different HDMI cable — some older cables don't support 4K @ 60Hz
5. The hub requires a USB-C port with DisplayPort Alt Mode support
