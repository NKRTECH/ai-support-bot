# Account Management

**Category**: Account  
**Last Updated**: July 2026  
**Applies To**: All SmartTech customer accounts

---

## Creating an Account

You can create a SmartTech account at **smarttech.in/register**. You'll need:
- A valid email address or mobile number
- A password (minimum 8 characters, must include a number and a special character)

You can also sign up using your Google account or OTP verification via your mobile number.

## Password Reset

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Go to **smarttech.in/login**
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Choose: reset via **email link** or **mobile OTP**
4. If email: check your inbox for a reset link (arrives within 5 minutes)
5. If OTP: enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile
6. Create a new password
7. Email reset links expire after 24 hours

If you don't receive the reset email or OTP:
- Check your spam/junk folder (for email)
- Make sure you're using the email/mobile you registered with
- Contact support at support@smarttech.in for manual password reset

## Updating Your Profile

You can update the following information from your account dashboard:

- **Name**: My Account > Profile > Edit Name
- **Email**: My Account > Profile > Change Email (requires OTP verification)
- **Mobile Number**: My Account > Profile > Change Mobile (requires OTP verification)
- **Shipping Addresses**: My Account > Addresses > Add/Edit/Delete
- **Payment Methods**: My Account > Saved Cards/UPI > Add/Edit/Delete
- **GST Number**: My Account > Profile > Add GSTIN (for business invoices)
- **Communication Preferences**: My Account > Notifications

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for added security:

1. Go to My Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication
2. Choose your method: Authenticator app (recommended) or SMS OTP
3. Follow the setup instructions
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place

If you lose access to your 2FA method, contact support with a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, PAN card, or Passport) for identity verification. Account recovery with ID verification takes 24-48 hours.

## Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete your SmartTech account:

1. Go to My Account > Settings > Delete Account
2. Enter your password to confirm
3. You'll have a **30-day grace period** to change your mind
4. After 30 days, all personal data is permanently deleted as per IT Act, 2000

**Before deleting**, make sure to:
- Download any invoices or order receipts you need (for GST/tax filing)
- Use any remaining store credit (it cannot be recovered after deletion)
- Cancel any active subscriptions or protection plans
