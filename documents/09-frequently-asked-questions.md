## Orders and Purchases

### How do I place an order?
Browse products at smarttech.in, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or sign in to your SmartTech account for faster checkout and order tracking.

### Can I modify my order after placing it?
You can modify or cancel your order within **1 hour** of placing it. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. To modify within the 1-hour window, go to My Account > Orders > Modify Order. For COD orders, you can cancel anytime before dispatch.

### How do I cancel an order?
If the order hasn't shipped yet, you can cancel it from My Account > Orders > Cancel Order. If it has already shipped, you'll need to wait for delivery and then initiate a return. For COD orders, you can also refuse delivery.

### Do you offer price matching?
Yes. If you find the same product at a lower price on Amazon.in, Flipkart, or Croma within 14 days of purchase, contact support with a screenshot of the competitor's listing. We'll refund the difference as store credit. Price matching does not apply to marketplace third-party sellers, clearance items, or bundle deals.

### Can I buy SmartTech products in physical stores?

- **Croma** stores (select locations)
- **Reliance Digital** stores (select locations)
- **Vijay Sales** (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR)

Visit smarttech.in/stores for locations near you.

## Products

### Are SmartTech laptops good for gaming?
The SmartTech GameStation 16 is specifically designed for gaming with a dedicated NVIDIA RTX 4070 GPU and a 240Hz display. The ProBook 15 can handle light gaming but is optimized for professional work. The AirBook 13 is not recommended for gaming.

### Can I upgrade the RAM or storage in my laptop?
- **ProBook 15**: RAM upgradable to 32 GB (2 SODIMM slots), SSD upgradable (1 M.2 slot)
- **AirBook 13**: RAM is soldered and NOT upgradable. SSD is upgradable (1 M.2 slot)
- **GameStation 16**: RAM upgradable to 64 GB (2 SODIMM slots), storage upgradable (2 M.2 slots)

Opening the laptop does **not** void your warranty, as long as no damage is caused during the upgrade. If you're not comfortable doing it yourself, you can get it done at any SmartTech Authorized Service Centre for a ₹500 service charge.

### What operating system do SmartTech laptops use?
All SmartTech laptops ship with Windows 11. The ProBook 15 includes Windows 11 Pro, while the AirBook 13 and GameStation 16 include Windows 11 Home. Linux is not officially supported, but many customers report success with Ubuntu and Fedora on our hardware.

### Do you sell refurbished products?
Yes. Certified Refurbished SmartTech products are available at smarttech.in/refurbished. All refurbished products are thoroughly tested, cleaned, and come with a 6-month warranty. They may have minor cosmetic imperfections but are functionally identical to new products. Refurbished products are typically 30-40% cheaper than new.

## Subscriptions and Services

### What is SmartTech Shield?
SmartTech Shield is our extended protection plan that provides additional warranty coverage beyond the standard manufacturer warranty. Plans start at ₹2,999 and can include accidental damage protection. See our Warranty page for full details.

### Can I cancel SmartTech Shield?
Yes. You can cancel within the first 30 days for a full refund. After 30 days, a prorated refund will be issued based on remaining coverage. Cancel from My Account > Subscriptions > SmartTech Shield > Cancel.

### Do you offer a student discount?
Yes. Students and teachers get **10% off** all SmartTech products. Verify your status through your .edu email address or upload your college ID card at smarttech.in/student. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions. Valid for students from UGC-recognized institutions.

## Privacy and Security

### How is my personal data protected?
SmartTech is compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). We encrypt all personal data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never sell your personal information to third parties. Full details in our Privacy Policy at smarttech.in/privacy.

### What data do you collect?
We collect only what's necessary to process your orders and provide support:
- Name, email, mobile number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Order history and support interactions
- Device diagnostics (only when you opt-in for remote troubleshooting)
- GSTIN (only if provided for business invoices)

### How do I request deletion of my data?
You can request data deletion by emailing privacy@smarttech.in or from My Account > Settings > Privacy > Request Data Deletion. We process requests within 30 days as per DPDPA requirements.
